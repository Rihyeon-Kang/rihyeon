Hello! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of California, Santa Barbara. My primary research interests broadly lie in labor and public economics. Currently, my work focuses on three areas : - The dynamic consequences of routine-biased technological change for labor markets and education, with particular attention to skill mismatch - The effects of quotas on entry barriers and their unintended consequences - Evaluations of tax programs
